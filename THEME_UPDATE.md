# Theme update

Open **More → Appearance** to choose Light, Dark, or System. The selected theme is stored in the browser under `fridge-chef-theme`. System tracks the device color scheme and updates without reloading. The original diet, cuisine, local database, Gemini backend, shopping, and planning features are unchanged. The service worker cache was bumped to v10 to refresh offline assets.

## Verify
1. Choose Dark: panels, recipe tiles, forms, navigation, and guides switch to dark colors.
2. Refresh and navigate between tabs: choice persists.
3. Choose Light: overrides a dark operating-system preference.
4. Choose System: follows OS preference; toggle OS appearance to confirm.
5. Check iPhone Home Screen PWA and browser after updating cached assets.
